Potion of Purification

Two herbs of one tier brew into one potion of that tier. Every refining attempt burns one potion. Brewing is instant, nothing to reveal.

Furnace

A furnace is built from ingredients of its own tier. Its tier caps the tier of ingredients you can refine in it, and a furnace hotter than the ingredient adds a success bonus. One firing per cooldown.

Refining

Ingredients of one type and tier plus a potion of that tier go into a furnace; on success one ingredient of the next tier comes out. The result is sealed until the next minute.

Crucible

Ten ingredients of one tier melt into fewer random ones of the same tier. Choosing an output category costs a few pieces. Each piece rolls for a tier up, and there is a risk of a tier down at high tiers. Sealed until the next minute.

Ritual table

Five ingredients by the recipe of the item are sealed into a ritual item of their tier. A small chance of one tier higher, and a roll for a mythic key bound to the item kind. Sealed until the next minute.

The soul

The closing rite of the opening act. Eight ritual items on eight pedestals are sealed into a soul: five required, three enhancers, a mythic key in the slot of its kind. Sealing is free. The soul carries the Cauldron's stream until the alchemist is summoned from it.

The stream

The Cauldron pours its steam into the stream; every soul claims its share by weight. Claims open at the hundredth soul; the pours before that are all claimable at once.

How to mine

One process per card, up to 32 addresses on it. Your keys stay with you: the card only ever sees public addresses. Starting submit price 0.0002 ETH, gas about 300k per submit with reveal.

alchemists-miner --net robinhood --box "ssh -p PORT root@HOST ./hb-miner-linux-x64" --miners-file miners.txt
